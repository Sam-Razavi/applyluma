Junior Backend Developer - Nordlys Software, Stockholm

About the role
You will join a team of six building the booking platform used by Nordic ferry operators.

Requirements
- You have a bachelor's degree in computer science or a related field.
- Python is required, and you must have worked with SQL databases.
- You are comfortable using Git.

Nice to have
- Experience with Docker is a plus.
- It is a plus if you have used a cloud platform such as AWS.

We offer flexible hours, a learning budget and an office near Slussen.

About Nordlys Software
Nordlys was founded in 2011 and has offices in Stockholm, Oslo and Tallinn. Our staff band plays at the summer party every year, the office has a rooftop terrace with a view of the harbour, and on Fridays we have breakfast together. Last year our customers carried more than four million passengers.
